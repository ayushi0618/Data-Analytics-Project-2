# Free Streamlit Community Cloud Deployment Guide

> **Important:** No Google Cloud, no billing, no auto-pay is used for this deploy. If a step asks for a credit card, STOP — that is the wrong service.

---

## 5 Browser Steps to Deploy Free

1. Go to **https://share.streamlit.io/** and sign in with GitHub (use the same `ayushi0618` account as the repository).
2. Click **"Create app"** (or **"New app"**).
3. Select your repository details:
   - **Repository:** `ayushi0618/Data-Analytics-Project-2`
   - **Branch:** `main`
   - **Main file path:** `streamlit_app/app.py`
4. Click **"Deploy!"** and wait for the build to finish (Streamlit automatically installs `streamlit`, `pandas`, and `matplotlib` from `requirements.txt`).
5. Open the live app URL generated by Streamlit (e.g. `https://data-analytics-project-2-xxxx.streamlit.app`), verify the Executive page shows GMV **R$ 13,221,498.11** and orders **96,478**, test all 4 pages, and copy your live URL.
